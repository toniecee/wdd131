Design Principles

Understanding Alignment, Proximity, & Contrast

Good design organizes visual elements into a clear visual hierarchy. By applying core design principles such as alignment, proximity, repetition, and contrast, web developers create interfaces that users can scan and understand effortlessly.

Remember

Always ensure foreground and background pairings meet WCAG AA and AAA accessibility standards for clear legibility.
